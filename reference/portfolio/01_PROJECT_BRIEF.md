## Core narrative
Sanskar does not position himself as a conventional software developer. The portfolio should show a person who starts with complex real-world/public problems, researches them, structures information, designs practical systems and uses technology—including AI—where it creates genuine value.

## Audience
- Political/public-sector leaders and organisations
- Strategy and communications teams
- Technology/AI collaborators
- Potential clients/employers
- People evaluating analytical and execution capability

## Desired impression
**Professional, intelligent, strategic, credible, human.**

The website should feel like a serious personal portfolio—not a generic "AI developer" landing page.

## Design principle
Technology should be visible through the work itself. Avoid excessive neon, terminal aesthetics, floating 3D objects, generic AI imagery and decorative motion.

## Key proof
Six real projects spanning:
- Political intelligence
- Electoral AI/data
- Civic technology
- Constituency systems
- Organisational automation
- Grassroots research/strategy
